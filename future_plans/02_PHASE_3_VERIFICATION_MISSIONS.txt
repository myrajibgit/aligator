PHASE 3 BLUEPRINT: MISSION VERIFICATION ENGINES (FITNESS & OCR STUDY)
================================================================================
Goal: Elevate daily missions from simple manual checklists to verified, anti-cheat
academic and fitness habit routines.

1. FITNESS MISSION: 30-MINUTE ACTIVITY WITH SENSOR CROSS-CHECK
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Design Decision (per approved plan):
  * Avoid intrusive battery-draining continuous camera ML tracking.
  * Utilize an in-app 30-minute timer cross-verified against Google Fit / Apple Health.

- Technical Workflow:
  1. Student taps 'Start 30-Min Workout' in the daily missions tab.
  2. Flutter app records workoutStartTime and begins a 30-minute countdown timer.
  3. Background service maintains countdown notifications.
  4. Upon timer completion, app invokes the 'health' Flutter package:
     - Queries HealthKit (iOS) or Health Connect / Google Fit (Android).
     - Samples step counts, active energy burned, or active minutes recorded
       during [workoutStartTime, workoutEndTime].
  5. Verification Threshold:
     - Minimum 300 steps or >=15 active minutes during the half-hour window.
  6. Success: Sets FitnessMission.status = 'completed', fitStepsVerified = true,
     and awards +30 XP.

2. STUDY VERIFICATION MISSION: PHOTO -> OCR -> QUESTION BANK QUIZ
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- User Journey:
  1. Student completes studying a chapter in their physical textbook or notebook.
  2. Student taps 'Verify Study Page' -> opens in-app camera with page-alignment box.
  3. Photo is captured and processed on-device (privacy-first: raw photo does not
     need to leave the phone unless flagged).

- On-Device OCR Pipeline (Google ML Kit):
  * Package: google_mlkit_text_recognition
  * InputImage is processed on background isolate.
  * Returns recognized text blocks and lines.

- Chapter Keyword Matching Algorithm:
  * App retrieves tagged chapter keywords for the student's enrolled subjects
    (e.g., Physics -> Chapter: Electricity -> ["resistor", "current", "ohm", "voltage", "amperage"]).
  * Text matching engine counts keyword density and TF-IDF relevance.
  * Identifies matched chapter with >85% confidence score.

- Instant Knowledge Check:
  * Once the chapter is identified, app queries /questionBank/{subjectId}/chapters/{chapterId}/questions.
  * Serves 3 to 5 targeted questions specifically from that matched chapter.
  * Correct answers above 60% unlocks the Study Mission badge and awards +80 XP.

- Anti-Cheat Countermeasures:
  * Daily quota: 1 study verification per subject per calendar day.
  * Image hash caching: Prevents photographing the exact same page repeatedly.
