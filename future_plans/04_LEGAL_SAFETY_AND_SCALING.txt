================================================================================
LEGAL, CHILD SAFETY, COMPLIANCE & INFRASTRUCTURE SCALING
================================================================================

1. AGE RESTRICTION & COMPLIANCE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Minimum Age: 13+ only for initial public rollout.
- Registration Guard:
  * Date of birth / age acknowledgment during Google Sign-In.
  * Users declaring under 13 are locked out and no data is retained.
- Regulatory Alignment:
  * COPPA (United States): Strict prohibition of collecting location or PII from under-13.
  * GDPR-K (European Union): Parental consent required for under-16/13.
  * DPDP Act (India): Specific requirements regarding processing minors' digital data.

2. LOCALITY & LOCATION PRIVACY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Coarse Locality Only:
  * We NEVER track live GPS coordinates or pin-drop locations.
  * "Area" is strictly derived from the registered municipality/city of the school.
  * Protects minors from physical location stalking or proximity tracking.

3. DATA RETENTION & MEDIA HYGIENE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Study Photos:
  * On-device OCR processes images locally.
  * Uploaded verification crops are stored in private Cloud Storage buckets with
    30-day automatic lifecycle expiration policies.
- Chat Logs:
  * Inactive chat logs older than 90 days are archived or purged.

4. BACKEND SCALING STRATEGY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Current (0 - 10,000 MAU):
  * Firebase Spark/Blaze Tier is virtually zero cost.
  * Firestore real-time listeners support low-latency DMs and party updates.
- Scaling (10,000 - 250,000 MAU):
  * Chat Layer: Migrate from Firestore document listeners to specialized real-time
    infrastructure (Firebase Realtime Database or WebSockets via Node/Go microservice).
  * Leaderboard Caching: Offload high-frequency weekly leaderboard reads to Redis
    sorted sets (ZADD / ZREVRANGE) to keep database read costs near zero.
================================================================================
